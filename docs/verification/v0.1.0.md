## Known limitations

- The package is unsigned and may trigger Windows reputation or SmartScreen warnings.
- Distribution is a portable self-contained ZIP, not an installer or auto-updater.
- The UI is the tested desktop shell; F1 25 UDP capture, replay, lap analysis, and coaching arrive in
  later milestones.
- Domain, protocol, and telemetry test assemblies are placeholders until those product slices begin.
- No public source-code license is granted by this package.
